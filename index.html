How to Play

  • 👆 Drag to move. A small drag sneaks quietly 🤫, a big drag runs
  • ⚡ Hold SPRINT to run really fast, until you're out of breath
  • 👁️ Ghosts see inside their cone of light. Walls and tombstones block it
  • ❓ When a ghost notices you, its ? fills up. Full means it chases you !
  • 🔊 Running makes noise and sprinting makes lots. Ghosts come to check noises
  • 🙈 Hide in bushes, coffins and crypts, but a ghost that sees you hide will find you
  • 🪨 THROW a pebble to make a noise and send ghosts the wrong way
  • 😱 Banshees are blind but hear everything, and scream for the ghosts
  • 🌫️ Phantoms float through walls and sense you nearby. They're slow, so keep moving
  • 🗺️ Your map (top right) shows where you've been. Tap it to make it bigger
  • ⭐ Stars: escape · get all the candy · never get spotted
  • ⌨️ Computer: arrows/WASD · hold X sneak · Shift sprint · Space hide · F throw · M map
